Workshops

Plan your future

Learning doesn't have to stop at the school gates. Alongside our work with schools and colleges, Come of Age runs hands-on workshops at community centres for young people who want to build real-life skills in a relaxed, welcoming space.

What to Expect

Our workshops are practical, not lecture-based. Instead of sitting through slides, you'll get stuck in. We give real hands-on experiences, developing practical skills in adult life today.
Each session is led by trained volunteers and experienced professionals, with plenty of time for questions and conversation. Everything you need is provided, and there's no pressure and no judgement.
