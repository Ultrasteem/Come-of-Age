Lectures

Our Service

Many of the most important lessons in adult life never make it onto the timetable. Come of Age works in partnership with schools to bring engaging, relevant talks directly to Year 10 and 11 students, helping them feel prepared for what comes after the classroom.
Our sessions are designed to hold attention, not test it. Speakers use real-life stories, live demonstrations, group discussions, quizzes, and Q&A to keep students involved from start to finish. Students are encouraged to ask questions, share opinions, and challenge ideas, so every session feels like a conversation rather than a lecture.

What we cover

Each talk focuses on practical knowledge that students will need in modern adult life, including:
Careers and employment: understanding the world of work, apprenticeships, further education options, and what employers look for.
Money matters: how wages, tax, savings, and debt work, plus the realities of budgeting and the cost of living.
Independent living: renting, household bills, home upkeep, and looking after yourself away from home.
Family and relationships: healthy relationships, responsibilities of parenthood, and where to find support.
Digital life: managing your online presence, spotting scams, and protecting your personal data.
