Come of Age

Plan your future

Come of Age is a non-profit organization dedicated to preparing school and college students for the realities of adult life. Too often, young people graduate with strong academic knowledge but little guidance on the everyday skills that independence demands. Come of Age exists to close that gap.

Our Mission

We are dedicated equip every young person with the practical knowledge, confidence, and resilience they need to thrive as independent adults, regardless of their background or circumstances. Come of Age partners with secondary schools, colleges, and universities to deliver hands-on sessions led by trained volunteers and industry professionals.
